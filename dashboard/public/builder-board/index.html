How to read this board
Community names, builders and submarkets are drawn from current builder and listing sources. Base prices, incentive terms, nightly rates, occupancy and rents are researched estimates for August 2026 and move weekly — confirm every number with the sales office and a lender before you write an offer. County geometry is real US Census data. Nothing here is a rate lock, an appraisal, or tax advice.
